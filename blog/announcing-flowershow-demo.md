**Welcome to the Flowershow Demo!** 🌺

We are excited to share our official **[Demo Site](https://demo.flowershow.app/)**.

We built this site to help you explore Flowershow's features and configuration options in a live, working environment. It demonstrates exactly what's possible when you turn your Obsidian vault into a website.

## What's Inside?

The demo site is a comprehensive showcase of Flowershow's capabilities:

*   **📚 Content Organization**: See how folders become navigation items and how sidebar structures work.
*   **📊 Data Rich Documents**: Explore examples of tables, charts, and other data visualizations powered by simple Markdown.
*   **📝 Blog & Authors**: Check out how a blog section looks and operates, including author profiles.
*   **⚙️ Configuration Options**: Every page you see is configured using standard Flowershow settings.

## How it Works

The best part? The entire demo site is open source.

You can browse the website to see the *result*, and then check the **[GitHub Repository](https://github.com/flowershow/demo)** to see the *source code* and Markdown files behind it.

It serves as a perfect reference implementation. If you wonder "How did they do that sidebar?" or "How is that chart configured?", just head to the repo and look at the corresponding file.
