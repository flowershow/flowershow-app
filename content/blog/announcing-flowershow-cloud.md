## What is Flowershow Cloud

If you're familiar with markdown and tools like Obsidian, you know their power and simplicity for storing your thoughts and knowledge.  But what happens when you want to publish your material for others?

That's where Flowershow Cloud comes in. Flowershow has already been a popular open-source option for publishing markdown and Obsidian notes for several years. But you have to deploy yourself and that can be a hassle. We wanted the simplicity of a cloud option to publish your vault online with nothing to install or manage yourself. And so we built Flowershow Cloud.

Flowershow Cloud let’s you publish markdown quickly and easily whether you're:

- Sharing your PKM (Personal Knowledge Management) system 
- Creating documentation
- Creating a wiki
- Building markdown-powered blog or website

# Fully featured

Flowershow Cloud understands your Obsidian content. Your published site will maintain:

- **\[\[Wiki Links\]\]**: Your internal links work exactly as they do in Obsidian  
- **Callouts**: From info and warning to tip and note blocks  
- **Code Blocks**: With the same syntax highlighting you're used to  
- **Math Equations**: Your LaTeX formulas render perfectly  
- **Mermaid graphs**: Your diagrams, flowcharts, and sequence diagrams render automatically  
- And more …
